## Architecture Features

### Universal Constraints Met
- ✅ **No primitives** - All use custom BufferGeometry or CatmullRomCurve3/TubeGeometry
- ✅ **No perfect symmetry** - All asymmetrical with unique visual personality
- ✅ **No flat meshes** - All have depth and volumetric structure
- ✅ **High negative space** - Open, airy designs allowing visibility
- ✅ **Kinetic motion** - All variants include smooth, continuous animation
- ✅ **Performance-safe** - Transform-only animation (no vertex mutation)
- ✅ **Material immutability** - Frozen/immutable materials prevent runtime mutation

### Material Philosophy
- **Emissive glow** indicates functional purpose and activity
- **Semi-translucent** (0.7-0.95 opacity) conveys flow and energy
- **Metallic qualities** (0.5-0.95 metalness) suggest precision/mechanics
- **Color-coded** by category for instant visual categorization

### Animation Patterns
1. **Orbital Motion**: Particles/nodes orbit around curves (`curve.getPoint(t)`)
2. **Oscillation**: Scale/rotation waves (`Math.sin/cos` with phase offset)
3. **Breathing**: Gentle expansion/contraction simulating respiration
4. **Wave Propagation**: Motion traveling down structures
5. **Counter-Rotation**: Opposing rotations suggesting balance/tension
