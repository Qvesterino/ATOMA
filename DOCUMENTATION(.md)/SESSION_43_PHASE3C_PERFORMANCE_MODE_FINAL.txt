HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

Data Flow (Per Frame):

  PersonalityVisualAdapter
    ↓ Generates 5 signals (0–1)
    ↓ Stores in node.userData.personalityVisual

  [NEW] FXPerformanceScaler_v1
    ↓ Reads signals & controller multipliers
    ↓ signal *= multiplier for each of 5 signals
    ↓ Clamps 0–1
    ↓ Writes back to SAME location

  PersonalityVFXLayer_v1
    ↓ Reads SCALED signals
    ↓ Applies CPU-side effects (automatically reduced)

  PersonalityShaderBridge_v1
    ↓ Reads SCALED signals
    ↓ Binds to uniforms (automatically scaled)

  PersonalityShaderEffects_Pack_v1
    ↓ Uses SCALED uniforms
    ↓ Applies effects (automatically muted when LowFX ON)

RESULT: All effects scale automatically without code changes

F7 Key Flow:

  Player presses F7
    ↓
  setupPerformanceMode() listener fires
    ↓
  fxPerformance.setLowFX(!current)
    ↓
  Multipliers updated (switch between sets)
    ↓
  Next frame: Scaler uses new multipliers
    ↓
  Visual effects scale immediately
