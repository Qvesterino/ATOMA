3. COMPONENT WEIGHTING BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

INTERNAL STABILITY (30% total weight):
    stability  × 0.50 = 15% of final score
    harmony    × 0.30 = 9% of final score
    clarity    × 0.20 = 6% of final score

ENERGY FACTOR (15% total weight):
    energy × 1.00 = 15% of final score

LOAD STRESS (15% total weight):
    (100 - load) × 1.00 = 15% of final score

CORRUPTION PENALTY (20% total weight):
    (100 - corruption) × 1.00 = 20% of final score

LINK QUALITY (20% total weight):
    avgLinkQuality × 0.70 = 14% of final score
    minLinkQuality × 0.30 = 6% of final score

10. QUALITY IMPROVEMENT SCENARIOS
================================================================================

Scenario 1: Isolated Node Gets Links
────────────────────────────────────

BEFORE:
  Node A (isolated)
  Quality: 50 (default, no links)
  Level: WEAK

AFTER (Node A gets 3 high-quality links):
  linkQualityAvg: 80
  linkQualityMin: 75
  linkComponent: (80×0.70 + 75×0.30) = 78.5
  
  New Score: 50×0.30 + 50×0.15 + 50×0.15 + 50×0.20 + 78.5×0.20
           = 15 + 7.5 + 7.5 + 10 + 15.7 = 55.7
  New Level: STABLE (+5.7 points, +1 level) ✓


Scenario 2: Corruption Attack
──────────────────────────────

BEFORE (Healthy):
  corruption: 5
  corruptionComponent: 95
  Score: 85
  Level: PRIME

AFTER (Sigma attack):
  corruption: 70
  corruptionComponent: 30
  Score: 30×0.30 + 50×0.15 + 50×0.15 + 30×0.20 + 50×0.20
       = 9 + 7.5 + 7.5 + 6 + 10 = 40
  Level: WEAK (-45 points, -2 levels) ⚠


Scenario 3: Node Load Balancing
────────────────────────────────

OVERLOADED (load = 0.9):
  loadComponent: 100 - 90 = 10
  Score: ~35
  Level: CRITICAL

REBALANCED (load = 0.3):
  loadComponent: 100 - 30 = 70
  Score: ~65
  Level: STABLE

Result: Rebalancing improves quality by +30 points ✓
