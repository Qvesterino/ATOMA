## Five-Component Enhancement

### 1. Motion-Synchronized Pulsing
```
Progress along link: 0 ──→ 0.5 ──→ 1.0

Brightness wave:    [dim] → [bright] → [dim]

Result: Particle pulses as it travels
Effect: Creates rhythm that helps eye track motion
```

### 2. Progressive Energy Intensity
```
Start: 0.7×  │  Mid: 0.85×  │  End: 1.0×

Brightness gradually increases
Shows energy accumulation along path
Indicates flow direction
```

### 3. Combined Energy Intensity
```
Motion wave  +  Progressive brightness  =  Readable effect

50% rhythm  +  50% directionality  =  Balanced, clear
```

### 4. Thickness Modulation
```
Particle size:  [small] ──→ [large] ──→ [small]

Swells at peaks, compresses at troughs
Creates "echo" visual effect
Adds another dimension of motion perception
```

### 5. Trail Visibility Envelope
```
Start: 1.0×  │  Mid: 1.2×  │  End: 1.0×

Particles brightest at midpoint
Emphasizes energy in transit
Gentle at edges (accelerating/dissipating)
```

**Color**: Red-tinted (base corruption color)

## Design Intent

### "Motion, Not Mutation"

Modulation should:
- ✅ Enhance perceived motion
- ✅ Communicate direction
- ✅ Show energy state
- ❌ NOT mutate appearance drastically
- ❌ NOT feel glitchy or artificial

### "Subtle, Not Flashy"

Enhancement should:
- ✅ Improve readability quietly
- ✅ Support network comprehension
- ✅ Feel intentional and polished
- ❌ NOT draw excessive attention
- ❌ NOT compete with nodes/links
