WHAT IS PERFORMANCE MODE?
================================================================================

LowFX Mode reduces the visual intensity of all Phase 3c personality effects
to improve performance on lower-end hardware while maintaining gameplay.

When LowFX is ON:
  • Personality signals are scaled down (clarity 0.4, resonance 0.4, etc)
  • Effects become subtler but still visible
  • CPU and GPU load reduced
  • Frame rate stabilizes
  • Player can toggle instantly with F7 key

When LowFX is OFF (default):
  • Full quality effects restored
  • Multipliers all 1.0
  • Rich, vivid personality visuals
  • Maximum visual fidelity

HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

Architecture:

  PersonalityVisualAdapter (Week 1)
    ↓ Generates 5 signals (clarity, resonance, entropy, focus, corruption)
    ↓ Writes to node.userData.personalityVisual

  [NEW] FXPerformanceScaler_v1
    ↓ Reads signals
    ↓ Multiplies by controller multipliers
    ↓ Writes back to SAME location

  PersonalityVFXLayer_v1 (Week 2)
    ↓ Reads SCALED signals
    ↓ Applies CPU-side effects

  PersonalityShaderBridge_v1 (Week 3)
    ↓ Reads SCALED signals
    ↓ Binds to uniforms

  PersonalityShaderEffects_Pack_v1 (Week 4)
    ↓ Uses SCALED uniforms
    ↓ Applies advanced effects

RESULT: All effects automatically respect performance multipliers

Multiplier Values (LowFX ON):

  clarity:           0.4  (60% reduction)
  resonance:         0.4  (60% reduction)
  entropy:           0.2  (80% reduction)
  focus:             0.3  (70% reduction)
  corruption:        0.5  (50% reduction)
  vfxIntensity:      0.3  (70% reduction)
  shaderIntensity:   0.25 (75% reduction)
