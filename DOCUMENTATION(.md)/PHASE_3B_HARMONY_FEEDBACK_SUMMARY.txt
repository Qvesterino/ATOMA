METHOD: applyHarmonyFeedback() [lines 1916–2006]
CHANGE TYPE: Enhanced existing method (non-breaking)

ENHANCEMENT: Added saturation dampening to harmony feedback loop

FORMULA:
  dampenedGain = healedAmount × 0.02 × (1 - harmony / 1.0)

Where:
  - healedAmount = fraction of corruption healed (0-1)
  - 0.02 = feedback factor (base harmony gain rate)
  - (1 - harmony / 1.0) = saturation multiplier (diminishing returns)

BEHAVIOR:
  harmony = 0.0  → 100% feedback effectiveness
  harmony = 0.5  → 50% feedback effectiveness
  harmony = 0.9  → 10% feedback effectiveness
  harmony = 1.0  → 0% feedback effectiveness (no further growth)
