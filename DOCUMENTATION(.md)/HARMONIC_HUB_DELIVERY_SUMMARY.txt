SYSTEM CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

HARMONIC MODES (Deterministic, No Randomness)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2 Links → MIRRORED
  Pattern: 0° and 180° phase offset
  Visual: Push-pull oscillation, energy alternates
  Use: Simple coordination, early network indicators

3-4 Links → STANDING WAVE
  Pattern: 120° (3-link) or 90° (4-link) spacing
  Visual: Energy cascades in sequence around hub
  Use: Medium complexity, visible wave patterns

5+ Links → ORBITAL
  Pattern: ±90° sinusoidal drift (~20s period)
  Visual: Smooth orbital dance, organic motion
  Use: High complexity, sophisticated coordination

HUB ACTIVATION CONDITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hub becomes harmonic center when:
  ✓ activeLinkCount >= 2
  ✓ avgSynergy >= 0.5
  ✓ harmony >= corruption × 1.2
  ✓ instability <= 0.4

Hub deactivates when conditions fail (smoothly, no sudden cutoff)

SYNC STRENGTH CALCULATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

strength = (synergy × 0.4)
         + (harmony × 0.3)
         - (corruption × 0.8)
         - (instability × 0.6)
         + (extraLinks × 0.1)

Result: 0 (no sync) to 1 (perfect sync)

network coordination.
