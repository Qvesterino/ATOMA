QUALITY COMPUTATION (4 Components, Weighted)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STRUCTURAL QUALITY (30% weight)
├─ Link validity check (0 if dead)
├─ Base: 80 points
├─ Distance penalty: (distance - 50 units) * 0.5 pts/unit
├─ Staleness penalty: -20 if > 5 seconds
└─ Result: 0-100

NODE HARMONY & STABILITY (40% weight)
├─ Average stability of both nodes (60% weight)
├─ Average harmony of both nodes (40% weight)
├─ Formula: stab*0.6 + harm*0.4
└─ Result: 0-100

LOAD QUALITY (15% weight)
├─ Average load ratio of both nodes (0-1)
├─ Convert to quality: 100 * (1 - average_load)
├─ High load = low quality
└─ Result: 0-100

CORRUPTION QUALITY (15% weight)
├─ Maximum corruption (worst node determines)
├─ Convert to quality: 100 - max_corruption
├─ Higher corruption = lower quality
└─ Result: 0-100

FINAL SCORE
finalScore = structural*0.30 + harmony*0.40 + load*0.15 + corruption*0.15
(Clamped to 0-100)
