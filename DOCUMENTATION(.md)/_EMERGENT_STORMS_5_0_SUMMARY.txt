   • Fragmented glyphs
   • Trigger: instability > 0.65, synergy < 0.5

3. Corruption Storm (corruption-dominant)
   • Inverted syntax loops (reverse orbits)
   • Red/orange glyph flashes
   • Distorted arc connections
   • Phase-flipped patterns
   • Trigger: corruption > 0.65

4. Ascended Storm (consciousness-dominant)
   • Diamond and lotus motifs
   • Perfect multi-layer spirals
   • Orbiting symbolic halos
   • Luminous white core
   • Trigger: clarity > 0.8, synergy > 0.7

TRIGGER CONDITIONS
──────────────────
Primary:
• thoughtDensity > 4.0
  (sum of chain lengths in 2-link radius)

Secondary:
• synergy > 0.75 across 3+ connected nodes
• corruption > 0.65
• harmony > 0.85

STORM ANATOMY
─────────────
Per storm:
• stormCore (1): Pulsing center sphere
• stormGlyphs (12-20): Orbiting symbols
• recursiveArcs (4-8): Curved connectors
• rippleMeshes (3-9): Expanding waves

Total meshes per storm: ~25-40
Max simultaneous: 8 storms = 200-320 meshes

ANIMATION DETAILS
─────────────────
Core Pulsing:
  pulseScale = 0.8 + sin(time × 3.0) × 0.4
  Result: Breathing/heartbeat effect

Orbital Motion:
  angle = baseAngle + time × 2.0 rad/s
  radius = 0.4 × (0.8 + sine(progress) × 0.2)
  Result: Smooth orchestrated movement

Glyph Breathing:
  scaleZ = 1.0 + sin(time × 2.0) × 0.1
  Result: Individual inflate/deflate

Arc Waves:
  midpoint.y += sin(time × 2.0) × 0.05
  Result: Snaking connectors

Ripple Expansion:
  scale = 1.0 + elapsedTime × 1.0
  opacity = 1.0 - (expansion / maxRadius)
  Result: Expanding fading circles
