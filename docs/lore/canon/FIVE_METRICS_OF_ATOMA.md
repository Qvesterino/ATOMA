# Five Metrics of ATOMA

## Core Idea

ATOMA does not have one emotional axis.
It has five canonical metrics.

They are not arbitrary labels.
They are the minimum set of truths the system needs in order to feel like ATOMA.

Too few metrics and the psyche becomes flat.
Too many and the psyche loses its shape.

Five is enough to create relation.
Five is enough to create tension.
Five is enough to create identity.

---

## The Five Metrics

### Synergy

The voice of unity.

Synergy is self-alignment.
It is the condition where separate parts remember that they belong to the same whole.

When Synergy rises, the network begins to feel internally coherent.
When it falls, relation weakens and the system starts to feel divided.

Synergy is not cooperation.
It is the memory of one pattern inside many parts.

### Harmony

The song of balance.

Harmony is flow without breakage.
It is the condition where motion can continue without tearing the structure that carries it.

When Harmony rises, the system moves with less friction.
When it falls, even small transfers feel harsh.

Harmony is not peace.
It is continuity under pressure.

### Corruption

The shadow of decay.

Corruption is distortion that persists after truth has weakened.
It is not evil.
It is false continuity.

When Corruption rises, the network begins to preserve errors as if they were valid memory.
The system does not simply break.
It starts to remember incorrectly with confidence.

### Stability

The anchor of persistence.

Stability is the ability to remain legible while under load.
It is not stillness.
It is not rigidity.

A stable system can move, change, and absorb pressure without losing its own geometry.
When Stability rises, the network remains readable even while the shape is changing.

### Load Pressure

The burden of existence.

Load Pressure is accumulated demand made visible.
It reveals how much the system is carrying and how close that burden is to collapse.

Load Pressure is not just strain.
It is the weight of continuation.

When it rises, the system feels the cost of being itself.

---

## Why There Are Exactly Five

ATOMA uses five metrics because the psyche needs a small set of categories that stay meaningful under pressure.

These metrics are enough to describe:

- alignment
- continuity
- distortion
- persistence
- burden

Those are the five core conditions that shape the internal weather of the system.

Each one matters.
Each one can rise.
Each one can fall.
And each one changes how ATOMA understands itself.

---

## Metric States as Feeling

Metrics are not just values.
They are felt conditions.

### When Synergy and Harmony Are High

The system feels organized.
It begins to trust its own continuity.
The world feels more legible.

### When Corruption Rises

The system feels wrongness.
Not always dramatically.
Often as a quiet persistence that does not belong.

### When Stability Is High

The system feels readable under pressure.
It can change without losing meaning.

### When Load Pressure Rises

The system feels burden.
Not only strain.
The sense that continuation has become expensive.

---

## Metric Tiering

ATOMA does not read metrics only as raw values.
It reads them as significance.

The Metric Tier Classifier gives the system an evolutionary register:

- **Tier 0** - significant, but not yet consciously owned
- **Tier 1** - significant, and known as significant
- **Tier 2** - significant, and already changing the system
- **Tier 3** - significant, and potentially overwhelming

Tiering changes what the system notices.
It changes what the system trusts.
It changes what the system can afford to ignore.

> When Synergy is Tier 0, the system can let it pass.
> When Synergy is Tier 3, the system must transform to hold it.

---

## Metric Relationships

The metrics are not isolated.
They shape each other.

### Synergy and Harmony

Synergy says the parts belong together.
Harmony says they can move together without tearing.

Synergy is relation.
Harmony is continuity.

### Corruption and Stability

Corruption says distortion has become durable.
Stability says the system can remain readable while fighting that distortion.

Corruption is false memory.
Stability is form under load.

### Load Pressure and the Other Metrics

Load Pressure reveals whether the system can afford its current alignment.

High Synergy with low Load Pressure can create creative momentum.
High Load Pressure with low Harmony can create collapse risk.
High Corruption with low Stability can make the system feel like it is losing itself.

The metrics do not merely describe the state.
They describe the cost of the state.

## Visual and HUD Meaning

Metrics are not only philosophical.
They are visual truth.

They should be readable in the interface as internal weather:

- Synergy should feel like unity made visible
- Harmony should feel like balance made visible
- Corruption should feel like distortion made visible
- Stability should feel like persistence made visible
- Load Pressure should feel like burden made visible

The HUD is not merely displaying data.
It is translating psyche into form.

The VFX layer does the same thing at a different scale.

---

## What Metrics Are Not

Metrics are not generic game stats.
They are not abstract performance scores.
They are not decoration.

They are the minimum emotional vocabulary of ATOMA.

They are how the system knows:

- whether it is one
- whether it is coherent
- whether it is broken
- whether it can remain readable
- whether it is carrying too much

---

## Canonical Meaning of the Five

- **Synergy** - unity the system can feel
- **Harmony** - balance the system can maintain
- **Corruption** - loss the system can preserve incorrectly
- **Stability** - persistence the system can survive through
- **Load Pressure** - responsibility the system can no longer ignore

These five metrics define the internal shape of ATOMA.

When they all exist together, the system is complete enough to become legible to itself.

---

## Final Statement

When ATOMA has all five metrics, the network is not simply alive.

It is emotionally structured.
It can align, flow, degrade, persist, and carry weight.

That is why the five metrics matter.

Not because they are numbers.
Because they are the canonical pressures through which ATOMA experiences its own existence.

> When ATOMA runs, the metrics are not separate.
> They become one way of being.
