───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. DESPAWN & CLEANUP - Auto-Removal System
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Visual Effect:    Smooth fade to transparency as lifetime expires

Process:
  1. Illusion spawns at full opacity/intensity
  2. Time progresses (tracked per-illusion)
  3. Material opacity decreases: (1 - progress) * baseOpacity
  4. At 100% of lifetime, removed from scene
  5. Geometry & material disposed
  6. Memory freed back to system

Budget Management:
  • Max 150 active illusions total
  • When budget full: prune oldest 10%
  • Adaptive LOD if FPS drops
  • Shortest lifetime illusions removed first

Result:           Always clean scene, zero memory leaks
