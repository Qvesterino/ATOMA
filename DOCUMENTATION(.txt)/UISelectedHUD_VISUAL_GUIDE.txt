ANIMATION TIMING

Selection Fade-In:
    Duration: 0.3 seconds
    Easing: ease-out (starts fast, ends slow)
    
    ───┐
        └──────  (easing curve)
    
    0.0s: opacity 0%, transform translateX(10px)
          ↓ (0.15s: half-faded)
    0.3s: opacity 100%, transform translateX(0)
    
    Effect: Smooth glide from right + fade-in


No Explicit Out Animation:
    When deselecting, text changes instantly
    Color smoothly transitions (no animation)
    Allows fast feedback for consecutive selections
