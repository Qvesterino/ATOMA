Energy Source:
  Link Synergy (0.5-0.8 per link) × 10 → Base energy
  Link Traffic (0-1 per link) × 5     → Bonus energy
  
  Total = (avgSynergy × 10) + (avgTraffic × 5)

Update Cycle (60 FPS):

Step 1: Read Data
  LinkingSystem.links[]
  └─ link.source (node reference)
  └─ link.target (node reference)
  └─ link.glowData.synergy (0.5-0.8)
  └─ link.traffic.load (0-1)

Step 2: Calculate Energy
  registry[nodeId].energy = (synergy × 10) + (traffic × 5)

Step 3: Determine Stage
  if (energy >= threshold):
    registry[nodeId].stage = newStage
    trigger burst effect

Step 4: Apply Mutations
  for each mutation in activeMutations:
    updateGlowVFX()    ← Mesh animation
    updateCoreVFX()    ← Mesh animation
    updateRingVFX()    ← Mesh animation
    updateParticleVFX()← Mesh animation
    updatePulseVFX()   ← Material animation
    updateColorVFX()   ← Material animation

Step 5: Render
  scene.render()
  └─ Original nodes (unchanged)
  └─ VFX overlays (updated each frame)
  └─ Burst effects (animated)
