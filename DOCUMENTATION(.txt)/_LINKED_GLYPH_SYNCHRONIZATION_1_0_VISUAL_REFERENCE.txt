WHAT SYNCHRONIZATION TELLS THE PLAYER:

Perfect Sync (≥70% synergy):
  "This is a strong, healthy connection"
  → Glyphs dance together perfectly
  → Visual: Unity, agreement, stability

Medium Sync (30-69% synergy):
  "This connection works, but with some friction"
  → Glyphs echo each other with slight lag
  → Visual: Cooperation, moderate stability

Loose Sync (<30% synergy):
  "This connection is fragile and deteriorating"
  → Glyphs barely acknowledge each other
  → Visual: Instability, disconnection, tension

Inverted (high corruption):
  "This connection is broken or hostile"
  → Glyphs move in opposition
  → Visual: Conflict, decay, opposition
