================================================================================
PHASE 3b OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Objective: Create self-reinforcing harmony growth where successful healing
          increases harmony on the involved links and nodes.

Mechanism:
- Healing corruption (Phase 3) triggers harmony gain (Phase 3b)
- Harmony gain = healedAmount × 0.02 (very small, 2%)
- Harmony capped at 1.0 (hard limit)
- Per-link 500ms cooldown (prevent spam)
- Cascade feedback reduced 50% (prevent exponential growth)

Result:
- Harmony becomes a self-evolving restorative force
- High-harmony zones grow stronger through use
- Creates emergent "healing hotspots"
- Corrupted territory can be gradually reclaimed

HARMONY FEEDBACK FORMULA
================================================================================

Local Healing (from Phase 3):
  healedAmount = min(linkData.level, |healingDelta|)
  harmonyGain = healedAmount × 0.02
  newHarmony = min(currentHarmony + harmonyGain, 1.0)

Cascade Healing (via Phase 3):
  cascadeHealing = strength × BASE_HEAL_RATE × 0.1
  healedAmount = min(linkLevel, cascadeHealing)
  harmonyGain = (healedAmount × 0.5) × 0.02  // Reduced by 50%
  newHarmony = min(currentHarmony + harmonyGain, 1.0)

Example Timeline (single link):
  T=0s:   Harmony=0.85, Corruption=0.5
  T=5s:   Harmony=0.85 (no gain yet), Corruption=0.4 (healing)
  T=5.5s: Harmony=0.851 (gain from healing), Corruption=0.4
  T=10s:  Harmony=0.852, Corruption=0.3
  T=30s:  Harmony=0.87, Corruption=0.0 (healed)
  T=31s:  Harmony=0.88 (bonus from full heal), Corruption=0.0
