# /review-atoma

## Goal
Validate the patch for safety and correctness.

## Steps
1. Switch to skill: `atoma-reviewer`
2. Load:
   - Audit
   - Plan
   - Patch diffs
3. Evaluate:
   - rule compliance
   - risk
   - visual and gameplay impact
4. Produce verdict:
   - APPROVE
   - APPROVE_WITH_RISK
   - REJECT
5. Save verdict to Focus Chain.
6. Output verdict and justification.
