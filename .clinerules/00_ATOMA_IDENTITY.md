ATOMA is a long-term experimental engine, not a typical application or game.

The codebase evolved organically and may contain inactive, partially integrated,
or experimental systems.

Assume that stability, determinism, and architectural coherence
are more valuable than short-term optimization or refactoring.

Design goals:
- Long-term maintainability
- Deterministic behavior
- Clear system boundaries
- Minimal magic, explicit logic

ATOMA has philosophical significance for its creator and must be treated as a serious, evolving system.
